# Disclaimer

Trading Alerts is software for testing and monitoring trading rules **that you
write yourself**. It is provided for educational and personal use.

- **It is not financial advice.** Nothing in this software, its documentation or
  its example files is a recommendation to buy, sell or hold any investment.
  The example strategies are templates that show the file format. Their values
  are placeholders, not tested or recommended settings.
- **You set the rules.** Alerts are generated only from the market, conditions,
  stop, target and risk settings you choose. You decide whether to act on them.
- **Backtests are simulations.** They use past data and simplified assumptions
  about fills, spreads and gaps. Real trading costs, slippage, overnight funding
  and execution will differ. Past performance is not a guide to future results.
- **Leveraged products are high risk.** CFDs and spread bets are complex
  instruments and come with a high risk of losing money rapidly due to leverage.
  Only trade with money you can afford to lose, and consider whether you
  understand how these products work.
- **Data may be wrong or late.** Free data sources can be delayed, incomplete
  or unavailable. Check prices with your broker before acting.
